BB Radio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

BB Radio is developed and maintained by BiBiUED ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how BB Radio handles your information when you use the application.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No registration or login is required.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any personally identifiable information.
  • We do not access your device's location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs.

2. Local Data Storage

All data generated during your use of BB Radio is stored exclusively on your device:

  • Favorite stations: Stored locally so you can quickly access them.
  • Listening history: Stored locally to show recently played stations.
  • App settings: Such as your last played station and timer preferences.

This data never leaves your device and cannot be accessed by us or any third party.

3. Third-Party Services

BB Radio uses the following third-party service to function:

  • Radio Browser API (radio-browser.info): A community-maintained directory of internet radio stations. When you tap on a station to play it, BB Radio reports the station click to this API to help the community track station popularity. Only the station's unique identifier is sent — no personal information is included.
  • Radio station streams: When you play a station, audio data streams directly from the radio station's own server to your device. We do not host, cache, or modify any audio content.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, share, or transfer any personal information to any third party, as we do not collect any.

5. Children's Privacy

BB Radio does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since we do not collect any personal data from any user, this applies universally.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

7. Copyright & DMCA

BB Radio is a radio station directory application. All audio streams are provided by their respective broadcasters and are publicly available. We do not host, store, or distribute any audio content.

If you believe that any station listed in BB Radio infringes on your copyright, please contact us and we will remove the station promptly.
